Lighting the studio

Drag the title to move the lighting controls. With the title focused, use the arrow keys to move, Shift for larger steps, or Home to return to the original position.

Choose a studio preset to change the workspace lighting. Adjust the vertical brightness control or drag left or right on a scene to dim or brighten it. Swipe down from the upper right to open the controls. Swipe up on the background or press Escape to close them. Drag the chair to roll it. Turn the monitor and stand together, or drag vertically to tilt the screen. Grab the mouse, keyboard, pen, pencil holder or journal to lift it. Release it to land on the desk. Grab the end of the keyboard to turn it. Brush the plants to move their leaves. Pull the blind down to dim the daylight or up to let it in. On narrow screens drag an empty part of the room sideways to reach the window. Use the up and down arrow keys to move between scenes, and the left and right arrows to adjust the light.
